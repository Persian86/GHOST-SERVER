Manual requests between updated Android phones. No SMS, calls, lights, sirens or automatic escalation in this stage. Open “Help & family alerts” once on each updated phone to register it here. Notification delivery depends on phone settings and connectivity.
Location is never substituted automatically. Configure only phones and recipients you trust. Removing a phone here immediately removes its access to request details.

Requests from the last 24 hours

“Sent to connection” is not confirmed phone receipt. Acknowledgement means someone pressed “I have seen this request”; it does not confirm help is on the way. Requests expire after one hour. Refresh does not resend alerts.

Break-in mode — Stage 7B

Selected controllers review and confirm a plan on their phone. Test mode simulates home actions and sends labelled TEST alerts to selected Ghost phones. Live mode uses the exact lights and duration-capable sirens below. Emergency calling remains a separate phone dialler action.
An empty target list gives family alerts only. Stop closes the request and attempts to stop its sirens; safety lights stay on. Saving setup closes any active request and attempts to stop its sirens. Use Home Assistant or the physical controls if a stop is unconfirmed.

Missed check-ins — Stage 7C

A person schedules one check-in on their phone. At the due time Ghost sends a private reminder; if they do not check in during the grace period, selected family phones receive an alert. No alarms, location sharing or calls. TEST sends real, labelled phone notifications.

Open Check-ins on each updated phone first. Saving setup cancels existing timers. Selected recipients can view the schedule and its status; a missed check-in does not prove an emergency.

Telephone conversation tests — Stage 7D

Ghost can make a short AI conversation test to a previously consenting Australian landline/mobile contact. The contact must press 1 before speech processing starts. No emergency calls, automatic escalation, recordings or household tool access. DRY RUN places no call and contacts no provider.

Front door

Doorbell presses, visitors and deliveries. Ghost looks, says who it is if Visual ID knows them (“Hayley's at the front door”), otherwise describes them, and tells you in the room you're in.
Find it in Home Assistant → Settings → Entities: search “visitor”, “ding”, “ring” or “doorbell” and press the bell to see which one changes.
Your phone and PC get it with the picture, the hub tablet pops up the live camera. Ask “who was at the door?” any time, or “test the doorbell”. A bell press always gets through Do Not Disturb.

Meal planner

Try: “plan dinners for the week”, “make Thursday tacos”, “what's for dinner?”, “put this week's dinners on the shopping list”, “how do I make tonight's dinner?”. Tonight's dinner shows on the kitchen tablet.

Seasonal themes

Ghost dresses up for the holidays — a witch's hat and bats for Halloween, a Santa hat and snow for Christmas, bunny ears for Easter, a party hat on birthdays — and the phone, tablet colours and greeting change with it.
Pick a preview to see a theme on every screen now; set it back to Automatic afterwards.

Ghost Hub tablet — home tiles

Leave empty and the tablet picks your lights and switches automatically. Tapping a tile switches lights, switches, fans and scenes. Locks, alarms and doors are shown but can't be operated from the tablet.

Do Not Disturb

Two quiet modes. Mine: your phone and PC go quiet. House: every device, the hub tablets too. Turn them on by voice (“don't disturb me until 7am”, “house quiet mode for an hour”), with the switch in each app, or on a schedule. Ghost holds announcements and gives a catch-up afterwards.
The phone adds its starred contacts automatically — star someone in Contacts and their calls ring through too.

Visual ID & photo frame

Faces are matched on this server only. Photos aren't kept, and nothing is stored about people who haven't been added.

Phone presence — automations

Standing rules

Routines

One request, a few familiar steps

Save a routine, then ask Ghost to start it by name or run it here. Nothing runs when you save. No schedules or location triggers are added. Home changes follow your household access policy and may pause for owner approval on Security.

New routine

Up to eight steps. Home control supports an exact light, fan or media player. No locks, alarms, arbitrary commands, scripts or nested routines.

Progress & recent runs

Cancel stops later steps; an action already sent may finish. Completed steps are not repeated on resume. If the server restarts during work, review the effects and cancel the interrupted run before starting again.

People profiles & recognition

Scans are optional. To enrol a face, use the tablet: Settings → Visual ID → Add a person (admin password required). Deleting a person removes their recognition samples, reply preferences and device assignments.

Personal replies & vocabulary

Conversation handoff

Off by default. Assign both devices to the same person below. Shared tablets and speakers are excluded. Ask Ghost to leave a recap on another device, then ask to accept it there within ten minutes. Nothing is announced remotely. Turning this off clears pending recaps.

Create a person without a scan

No camera or microphone enrolment is required. Existing recognised people are available too.

Personal device assignments

Choose whose reply preferences apply to each personal device. Leave rooms/shared tablets unassigned. This is not a login or a permission change; face and voice matches never select a private profile automatically.

Recently seen

Household access

Off keeps existing behaviour. Owner/Adult devices retain household access; Child/Guest devices get general conversation, weather, news and reasoning only. Mark your personal device Owner before enabling. New devices paired while enabled start as Guest.
A profile is the ceiling for everyone using that device. Face/voice recognition cannot raise it. It does not erase cached/offline data: configure a device before handing it to someone else.

Actions awaiting owner approval

When household access is on, remote device actions and Home Assistant changes need owner approval. Review the exact request below. Approving does not execute it: retry the same action from the requesting device within five minutes. Each approval works once.

Two-step login

Signing in needs your password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password…).

Lockout

5 wrong passwords or codes lock the admin page for 15 minutes. 5 wrong pairing codes pause pairing for 10 minutes.

Auto-lock

The admin page signs itself out and hides everything after a while untouched. Background updates don't count as use.

Signed-in browsers

Waiting for approval

New devices can't do anything until you approve them here.
Empty = your devices from Do Not Disturb → My devices (or every phone and PC if none are named). The phone asks for the fingerprint first.

Device keys

Each device swaps its key for a fresh one every 30 days. The apps keep theirs sealed in Android Keystore or Windows DPAPI.

Security alerts

Failed logins and lockouts · a new device asks to join · a device key is used from a new address. Sent to your own phone (they get through Do Not Disturb).

HTTPS (encrypted Wi-Fi traffic)

Devices fetch the server's own certificate once and from then on only talk to it over HTTPS, accepting only that certificate.
